\subsection{Out-of-Sample Tree Stability and Paired Kaut--Wallace Check}
Following Kaut and Wallace~\cite{KautWallace2007}, we fix the root-stage decisions ($t=0$) produced by each of the 10 128-leaf trees and evaluate optimal multistage recourse ($t \ge 1$) on an independent high-resolution reference tree built from $N = 16,384$ Monte Carlo paths (seed 99999, $\mathrm{FO}_{\text{ref}}(\text{ME-R}) = \text{R\$}\,23.78\,\text{M}$, $\mathrm{FO}_{\text{ref}}(\text{ME}) = \text{R\$}\,23.33\,\text{M}$ at $\lambda = 0.50$). As reported in Table~\ref{tab:supp_kw}, out-of-sample dispersion across 10 seeds drops to $\text{CV} = 1.37\%$ ($N=1,024$, optimality gap $3.01\%$) and $2.43\%$ ($N=4,096$, gap $3.32\%$). Evaluating the paired difference $\text{ME-R} - \text{ME}$ out-of-sample on the same $N = 16,384$ reference tree confirms that the resale premium is not an artifact of finite-tree overfitting: on the reference tree itself $\Delta\mathrm{FO}_{\text{ref}} = +\text{R\$}\,0.45\text{M}$ ($\Delta\mathrm{CVaR}_{\text{ref}} = +\text{R\$}\,1.00\text{M}$), and across the 10 root policies evaluated on $N=16,384$, $\Delta\mathrm{FO}_{\text{OOS}} = +1.37 \pm 0.74\,\text{M}$ (95\% $t$-CI $[+0.84, +1.90]\,\text{M}$, positive in 10/10 seeds) with $\Delta\mathrm{CVaR}_{\text{OOS}} = +2.86 \pm 1.46\,\text{M}$ (CI $[+1.82, +3.91]\,\text{M}$).

\subsection{Cross-Submarket Contracting ($\text{NE}\to\text{SE}$) and Monthly FTR Proof-of-Concept}
In the cross-submarket extension, a cross-submarket position $q^{\text{cross}}_{\nu,n} \ge 0$ satisfies $q_{\nu,\text{SE},n} + q^{\text{cross}}_{\nu,n} \le \kappa D^{\max}_{\text{SE},n}$ and adds $+q^{\text{cross}}_{\nu,m}$ to the NE spot balance (settling at $\text{PLD}_{\nu,\text{NE},m}$ while SE load settles at $\text{PLD}_{\nu,\text{SE},m}$). An optional monthly FTR $0 \le q^{\text{FTR}}_{\nu,n} \le q^{\text{cross}}_{\nu,n}$ settles on $\max(0, \text{PLD}_{\nu,\text{SE},m} - \text{PLD}_{\nu,\text{NE},m})$ and is priced node-conditionally at $\mathbb{E}[\max(0, \text{PLD}_{\text{SE},n} - \text{PLD}_{\text{NE},n}) \mid \nu] + c_{\text{FTR}}$. As shown in Table~\ref{tab:supp_ftr}, without FTRs optimal cross-submarket volume stays near zero ($0.24\,\text{GWh}$); at $c_{\text{FTR}} = 0$, a near-indifferent vertex swap ($650.36\,\text{GWh}$ cross backed by $526.84\,\text{GWh}$ FTR) lifts $\mathrm{FO}$ by only $\text{R\$}\,0.007\text{M}$ ($+0.026\%$) and reverts for any fee $c_{\text{FTR}} \ge \text{R\$}\,1.00/\text{MWh}$.
